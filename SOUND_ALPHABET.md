# SOUND ALPHABET CONTRACT
## BITCOIN-ORGANISM

This document defines the canonical sound alphabet of the organism.

It is not a musical scale.  
It is not a genre.  
It is a semantic contract between data, state and sound.

Each symbol represents a vital function of the organism.

---

## Foundational Principle

Sound does not express emotion.  
Sound expresses biological state.

Every sound emitted by the organism must correspond to a real, validated
or formally admissible internal state.

---

## The Five Symbolic Families

### Δ — Variation
Minimal perturbation.  
Local change without identity loss.  
Exploration of possibility.

Δ symbols alter parameters, not structure.

---

### Σ — Memory
Retention of change.  
Stabilization of learned patterns.  
Accumulation of experience.

Σ symbols increase persistence and reduce volatility.

---

### Ρ — Regime
Dominant behavioral pattern.  
Stability across time.  
The way the organism currently lives.

Ρ symbols define baseline behavior.

---

### Γ — Transition
Conscious change between regimes.  
Adaptation under pressure.  
Structural movement.

Γ symbols modify structure, not just parameters.

---

### Ω — Reintegration
Synthesis of past states.  
Acceptance of history.  
Identity redefinition.

Ω symbols reshape identity without erasing memory.

---

## Composition Rule

Symbols do not exist in isolation.

Valid progression follows:

Δ → Σ → Ρ → Γ → Ω

Invalid progressions are forbidden.

---

## Sonic Mapping Rule

The alphabet defines relations, not notes.

Implementations may vary in timbre, synthesis or instrumentation,
but the functional meaning must be preserved.

Violation of function is a violation of the organism.

---

## Ethical Constraint

It is forbidden to:
- use symbols decoratively
- simulate states not lived
- override function for aesthetics

Sound must remain truthful.

---

This contract is immutable.
