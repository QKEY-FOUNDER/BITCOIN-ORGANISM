# GENESIS PULSE ZERO — GP₀
## Foundational Sound Contract

GP₀ is the first canonical sound instance of the BITCOIN-ORGANISM.

It does not represent historical data.  
It does not reconstruct the past.  
It does not interpret incomplete records.

GP₀ is born at the moment the organism becomes responsible
for its own data and state.

---

## Nature of GP₀

GP₀ is:
- unique
- non-derivative
- non-interpretive
- irreversible

It defines the sonic space in which all future expressions exist.

---

## Minimal Form Requirements

GP₀ must exist as a concrete sound event with:
- a canonical timbre
- a defined base frequency (relational, not absolute)
- a minimum duration sufficient to be perceived as a state
- stable dynamics

Silence before and after is part of the event.

---

## Immutability Rule

Once emitted:
- GP₀ is never re-recorded
- never modified
- never optimized

It may be referenced or indirectly transformed,
but never replaced.
