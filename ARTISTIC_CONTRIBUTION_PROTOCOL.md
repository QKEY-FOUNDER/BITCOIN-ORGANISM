# ARTISTIC CONTRIBUTION PROTOCOL

## Purpose

BITCOIN-ORGANISM is not only a scientific or analytical system.  
It is also a sonic organism.

This protocol defines how data becomes state, and how state becomes sound, allowing artists, musicians and sound researchers to contribute without breaking the biological integrity of the system.

Art here is not decoration.  
It is perception used as measurement.

---

## Core Principle

No sound is arbitrary.

Every sound must emerge from the following irreversible chain:

Data → State → Expression

If a sound cannot be traced back to data through a canonical state, it does not belong to the organism.

---

## The Transformation Chain

### 1. Data Layer — Raw Reality

Primary data sources include:

- OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume)
- BTC Dominance
- Geographical market activity (GEO Engine)
- Temporal continuity (daily heartbeat)

These elements describe what happened.

Artistic contributions never modify raw data.

---

### 2. State Layer — Biological Interpretation

Raw data is interpreted into canonical biological states, such as:

- Volatility regimes
- Stress and recovery conditions
- Dominance balance
- Geographical silence or activity
- Immune posture (observe, protect, adapt)

This layer answers how the organism feels.

Artistic logic must map from state, never directly from raw data.

---

### 3. Expression Layer — Sound

Sound is the sensory projection of biological state.

Canonical mappings include, but are not limited to:

- Volatility → rhythm and temporal density
- Volume → amplitude and intensity
- Dominance → harmonic gravity and bass weight
- GEO activity → spatialization and harmonic distribution
- Immune response → timbre hardness or softness

Artists may propose new mappings, provided they remain:

- Deterministic
- Reproducible
- State-driven

Randomness is only allowed when biologically justified.

---

## What Artists Can Contribute

Artists may contribute:

- Sound synthesis engines
- Instrument definitions
- State-to-sound mapping functions
- Alternative tunings and scales
- Temporal interpretation models (slow time, fast time, memory-based time)

Artists may not:

- Alter historical data
- Override canonical state computation
- Introduce purely aesthetic randomness

Freedom exists inside constraint, not outside it.

---

## Contribution Structure

Recommended structure for artistic contributions:

- engine/
- sound_engine/
- instruments/
- mappings/
- renderers/

Each contribution should include:

- A clear description of the mapping logic
- Explicit reference to biological states used
- Explanation of musical intent
- Example output (audio or notation, if applicable)

This organism exists by love.

Every musician, composer, sound designer or researcher who recognizes love as a fundamental force is welcome to participate in the evolution of this infinite music.

---

## Final Constraint

If a contribution makes Bitcoin sound like something it never was, it does not belong here.

If it reveals something Bitcoin already was, but we had not yet heard,  
then the organism accepts it.
